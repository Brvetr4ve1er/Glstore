# 04 — Competitive Analysis

> Not to copy — to mark the floor and the ceiling. The floor is what
> the Algerian anime-streetwear segment looks like today. The ceiling
> is what the global category has normalised. SHINOBI's job is to
> clear the floor at launch and reach for the ceiling in 12 months.

## C. What we steal

### From Geekio
- **Bio-link funnel** — a hub that drives every IG visitor to one
  current campaign + the Custom Builder. We replicate it as
  `/`-as-Linktree-hub during drop weeks.
- **Pricing in the open** — no "DM for price" cliché.

### From NexWear
- **"Pass by the shop" energy** — turn the Bab Ezzouar presence into
  copy + photography on `/boutique`; not a bolted-on contact page.

### From AY-Line
- **Bilingual store** done right — locale toggle, currency, content
  parity. We hit launch with FR + EN.
- **Real product pages** — clean PDP with sticky add-to-bag on
  mobile.

### From OKAMI (sister project)
- **Drop engine** — scheduled launches with countdowns, notify-me,
  archive. Already specced in the OKAMI rebuild; reused here.
- **Track-by-phone+number** — already shipped in the Glstore backend.

### From Redbubble / TeePublic
- **The Custom Builder UX** — product chooser, design gallery,
  side(s), size, live price, preview, save-quote.
- **Search by character** — typeahead with anime franchises and
  character names.

## D. What we do *not* steal

| From | What | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| Redbubble | An infinite design library | SHINOBI is curated; the founder's taste is part of the product. |
| OKAMI | Drops as the *primary* lens | SHINOBI is half ready-made / half custom-print. Drops sit alongside, not on top. |
| AY-Line | Diaspora-first checkout | SHINOBI is Algeria-first. COD default, DZD pricing. |
| Geekio | "Order via DM" as the only path | We move DMs to web on purpose. |

## E. What we do that none of them do

| Capability | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| **Custom Builder + live price** from the owner's published table | Codifies SHINOBI's actual differentiator; removes the 7-message DM. |
| **In-store pickup as a first-class fulfilment** | Honours the boutique presence; cuts delivery cost on the buyer's side. |
| **Bilingual FR / EN at launch, AR + RTL in v1.1** | Real market segmentation; nobody else does AR well. |
| **WhatsApp + DM deep-link on every order confirmation** | Matches the actual local communication channel. |
| **Reviews with photo upload + verified-buyer flag** | First in the local segment to do it on PDP. |
| **`LocalBusiness` JSON-LD on `/boutique`** | Google Maps + Knowledge Panel for "boutique anime Alger". |
| **Search by anime + character** | Native to how the audience shops. |
